# 053: Review in the dashboard

Spec: docs/specification/ (sections 4 UC-009, 9 Review)
Depends on: 052

## What to build

The Review screen lists the queue, and an item page lets a reviewer approve or reject with a note.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Review list shows each item's page, the classifier's choice or the extracted fields, and the confidence.
- [ ] A classification item shows its page and choice; an extraction item shows each field with its citation and verified flag.
- [ ] Approve or Reject, with an optional note and the reviewer's name, records the decision, and the item leaves the list.
