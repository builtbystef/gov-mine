Description

The Building Commissioning (BCx) Consultant will provide comprehensive commissioning services throughout the design, construction, testing, and project closeout phases to ensure all building systems are planned, installed, tested, and operated in accordance with the Owner's Project Requirements (OPR), project specifications, and performance objectives. The Consultant will lead the commissioning process, including development of the Commissioning Plan, commissioning schedules and milestones, OPR reviews, commissioning meetings, functional and integrated systems testing, and ongoing verification of commissioning progress through substantial performance.
The successful proponent will also be responsible for managing commissioning documentation, coordinating and supporting training and handover activities, reviewing closeout and warranty documentation, and preparing final commissioning and Integrated Systems Testing (IST) reports. Services will extend through occupancy and closeout to include seasonal and deferred testing, follow-up site visits, and operational readiness support to facilitate a smooth transition to the Owner's operations team
