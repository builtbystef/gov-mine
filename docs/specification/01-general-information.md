# 1. General Information

- Description: gov-mine crawls a public institution's website from its homepage, keeps a copy
  of every page and file, works out which pages are worth keeping, extracts typed records
  (projects, news, meetings, contacts, RFPs, contracts) with a verified citation for every
  field, and re-runs on a schedule so that changes, corrections and removals become history.
  The first institution is CreateTO (createto.ca), the City of Toronto's real estate agency.
- Last updated: 2026-10-27.
